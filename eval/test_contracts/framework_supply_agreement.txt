GAS SUPPLY FRAMEWORK AGREEMENT

This Gas Supply Agreement (the "Agreement") is made between Northwind
Production Partners, LP ("Supplier") and Delta City Gas Authority ("Customer").

1. BACKGROUND

1.1 This Agreement sets out the framework under which Supplier will supply and
Customer will purchase Natural Gas from time to time.

2. SUPPLY

2.1 Supplier shall make Natural Gas available for delivery on a best-efforts
basis in accordance with nominations submitted by Customer.

3. PRICING

3.1 The price for Natural Gas shall be agreed between the parties at the time
of each nomination, subject to market conditions.

4. INVOICING AND PAYMENT

4.1 Supplier shall invoice Customer for delivered volumes. Customer shall pay
the invoiced amount within thirty (30) days of receipt.

5. QUALITY

5.1 Natural Gas shall meet the specifications attached as Schedule A.

6. FORCE MAJEURE

6.1 Neither party shall be liable for failure to perform required by an event
outside its reasonable control, subject to prompt notice.

7. TERM AND TERMINATION

7.1 This Agreement shall continue until terminated by either party on sixty
(60) days' written notice to the other.

8. LIABILITY

8.1 Each party shall be liable to the other for direct damages only.

9. CONFIDENTIALITY

9.1 The parties shall keep the terms of this Agreement confidential.

10. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES

10.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of Texas. Disputes shall be
referred to arbitration.
