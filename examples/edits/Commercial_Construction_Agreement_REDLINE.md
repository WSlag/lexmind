## EDIT 1 — Clause 20 LIMITATION OF LIABILITY  *(CRITICAL)*

**Issue:** Liability cap does not clearly apply to the indemnity in clause 21.

**Change:** Replace clause 20. The cap remains Contractor-only (as in the original) but is now expressly expressed to apply to claims "under or in connection with this Agreement, including under the indemnity in clause 21", and the consequential-loss exclusion remains mutual.

**Clause 20 (amended):**

> **20. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY**
>
> Except for liability arising from death or personal injury, fraud, wilful misconduct, or any liability that cannot be excluded or limited by law:
> (a) neither Party shall be liable to the other for any indirect, special, incidental or consequential loss or damage; and
> (b) the Contractor's aggregate liability under or in connection with this Agreement (including under the indemnity in clause 21) shall not exceed the Contract Sum.
>
> The limitations in this clause apply to all claims howsoever arising, including claims in contract, tort (including negligence), equity, under statute or otherwise.

## EDIT 3 — New Clause 15A PERFORMANCE SECURITY  *(CRITICAL)*

**Issue:** No performance security on a $3.85m commercial project.

**Change:** Insert new clause 15A immediately after clause 15 (INSURANCE).

> **15A. PERFORMANCE SECURITY**
>
> (a) Within 10 business days of the Effective Date, the Contractor shall provide the Owner with an unconditional bank guarantee (or insurance bond) for 5% of the Contract Sum from an Australian bank acceptable to the Owner.
> (b) The security shall be reduced to 2.5% of the Contract Sum upon the issue of the Notice of Practical Completion and shall be released within 14 days after the end of the Defects Liability Period, **provided there are no outstanding defects as certified by the Owner's project representative**.
> (c) The Owner may call on the security to recover any amount properly due from the Contractor under this Agreement.

## EDIT 5 — Clause 8 + New Clause 10A LIQUIDATED DAMAGES  *(CRITICAL — upgraded)*

**Issue:** No liquidated damages regime, AND the original draft proposed an LD clause referencing a "Date for Practical Completion" that the agreement never defines.

**Change (two parts):**

**(1) Clause 8 — make completion a firm obligation.** Replace the "estimated completion" wording with a defined date so the LD regime has a workable trigger:

> **8. CONSTRUCTION PROGRAMME**
>
> The Date for Practical Completion is **31 July 2027** (as adjusted under this Agreement). The Contractor shall use its best endeavours to achieve Practical Completion by that date. Commencement is estimated as 1 September 2026; construction duration is 11 months.

**(2) Insert new clause 10A immediately after clause 10 (DELAYS):**

> **10A. LIQUIDATED DAMAGES**
>
> If the Contractor fails to achieve Practical Completion by the Date for Practical Completion (as adjusted under this Agreement), the Contractor shall pay the Owner liquidated damages at the rate of **$3,000 per calendar day** (calculated to cover the Owner's re-tendering, financing and holding costs) up to a maximum of **10% of the Contract Sum**. The parties agree that this amount is a genuine pre-estimate of the loss the Owner is likely to suffer and is not a penalty.

## EDIT 6 — New Clause 5A SUSPENSION FOR NON-PAYMENT  *(HIGH)*

**Issue:** No suspension right for non-payment.

**Change:** Insert new clause 5A immediately after clause 5:

> **5A. SUSPENSION FOR NON-PAYMENT**
>
> If the Owner fails to pay any amount properly due under this Agreement within 14 days after the due date, the Contractor may, after giving 7 days' written notice, suspend the whole or any part of the Works until payment is received. The Contractor shall be entitled to an Extension of Time and reasonable prolongation costs arising from any such suspension. This clause does not limit any statutory right of suspension.

**Issue:** Vague "statutory adjustments" language.

This amount includes all labour, materials, equipment, project management, site supervision, standard permits, Contractor overheads and profit.
