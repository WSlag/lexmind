GAS SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Gas Supply Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into as of the Effective
Date by and between Astra Energy Trading, LLC, a limited liability company
("Seller"), and Meridian Utilities Group, Inc., a corporation ("Buyer").

1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Contract Price" means the price per MMBtu of Natural Gas as set forth in
Section 3.1, subject to adjustment as provided in this Agreement.

1.2 "Delivery Point" means the interconnected pipeline receipt point at the
Seller's designated facility.

1.3 "Term" means the period set forth in Section 2.

2. TERM

2.1 This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for a
period of thirty-six (36) months unless earlier terminated in accordance with
Section 8.

3. PRICING AND PAYMENT

3.1 Contract Price. Buyer shall pay Seller the Contract Price per MMBtu for
all Natural Gas delivered and accepted under this Agreement. The Contract
Price shall be indexed to a published monthly gas price index.

3.2 Payment Terms. Seller shall invoice Buyer monthly. Buyer shall pay all
undisputed amounts within fifteen (15) days of receipt of the invoice. Late
payment shall accrue interest at a rate of one percent (1%) per month.

4. DELIVERY

4.1 Seller shall deliver Natural Gas to the Delivery Point in accordance with
the monthly nomination schedule provided by Buyer.

5. TAKE-OR-PAY

5.1 Buyer shall pay for a minimum annual quantity of Natural Gas whether or
not delivered, unless the failure to take is caused by Seller's default or
an event of Force Majeure.

6. CUSTOMER QUALITY

6.1 All Natural Gas delivered shall meet the quality specifications set forth
in Exhibit A.

7. FORCE MAJEURE

7.1 Neither party shall be liable for failure to perform its obligations to
the extent such failure is caused by an event of Force Majeure, provided the
affected party gives prompt written notice to the other party.

8. TERMINATION

8.1 Upon the occurrence of a fundamental breach, the non-defaulting party
may terminate this Agreement upon thirty (30) days' written notice.

9. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

9.1 To the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, neither party shall be
liable to the other for consequential, incidental, or indirect damages.

10. CONFIDENTIALITY

10.1 Each party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to keep the terms
of this Agreement confidential.

11. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

11.1 Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be resolved by
arbitration in accordance with the rules of an international arbitral body.

12. GOVERNING LAW

12.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the
laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws
principles.
